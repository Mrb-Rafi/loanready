## Data

All business data in this repository (Rina's Bakery: transactions, invoices, bills,
receipts, counterparties, the injected test texts) is **synthetic**, generated by our own
scripts (`datagen/`, seed 20261014). Identifiers are reserved fakes: `.test` e-mail
addresses, `+999` phone numbers and `SBX-` bank accounts. The financial products in
`data/products.json` are fictional.
